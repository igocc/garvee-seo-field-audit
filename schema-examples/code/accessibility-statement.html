Accessibility Statement - GARVEE

Source: https://www.garvee.com/pages/accessibility-statement

Accessibility Statement We believe that every person has the right to live with dignity, equality, comfort, and independence. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying relevant accessibility standards to achieve these goals. We are committed to providing a website that is accessible in accordance with applicable laws, including the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), and we strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. Our Commitment We strive to make our website accessible to everybody, including individuals who use assistive technologies. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we are continuously taking steps to improve the accessibility and usability of our website. Accessibility Features To help achieve and maintain accessibility standards, our website includes the following features: Text Alternatives: We provide alternative text for non-text content where appropriate. Adaptable Content: Our content is structured to be accessible across devices and assistive technologies. Keyboard Navigation: We strive to ensure that key website functions are accessible via keyboard navigation. Readability: We aim to maintain clear structure, readable fonts, and sufficient color contrast. Ongoing Efforts We view accessibility as an ongoing effort and are continuously working to improve the accessibility and usability of our website. While we strive to adhere to accepted accessibility standards, some content may not yet fully comply with the strictest accessibility standards. Assistive Technology and Browser Compatibility Our website is designed to be compatible with commonly used assistive technologies and modern web browsers. However, we cannot guarantee full compatibility with all assistive technologies or browser versions, and performance may vary depending on the specific technology used. Third-Party Content Some areas of our website may include third-party content or functionality that is not fully controlled by Garvee. While we encourage third-party providers to ensure accessibility, we are not responsible for the accessibility of third-party content and cannot guarantee their compliance. If you encounter issues with any third-party content, please contact us so we can advocate for improvements with those providers. Updates to Our Accessibility Statement This Accessibility Statement is reviewed and updated periodically to reflect our ongoing commitment to accessibility. Feedback We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you are having trouble with any content on Garvee.com, or require assistance with any part of our site, please contact us. We are here to ensure you have full and equal access to our goods and services. Email Support: Please contact us at support@garvee.com with the Subject Line “Accessibility Inquiry.” Please include a description of the issue and the relevant page URL. We aim to respond to all accessibility-related inquiries within 24 business hours. Scheduled Call-Back Service: If you prefer to speak with a representative or require manual assistance, please email us your phone number and your preferred time window (between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM EST, Mon-Fri). We will call you back to provide personalized guidance, read product information, or manually process your order. We are committed to working with individuals with disabilities to ensure access to our goods and services through alternative communication methods where necessary.
