Payments Policy - GARVEE

Source: https://www.garvee.com/pages/payments-policy

Payments Policy Payment Methods Accepted: GARVEE accept payments via PayPal (direct and payment cards), Direct Credit Card and Klarna. Payment by PayPal If PayPal is available in your country, you can use PayPal. You need to create PayPal account that will be connected to your bank account via your debit card or credit card, and verified by PayPal. Payment by Credit Card At the GARVEE website, you can pay for your order directly using credit cards such as "Visa," "MasterCard," or other major credit card brands. Paying by credit card is a straightforward and secure method. Our Credit Card Payment Channel offers a comprehensive and convenient gateway that enhances the overall customer experience. The credit card payment processor we employ ensures the safest way to make a purchase. It utilizes the latest Encryption Technology, including 128-bit SSL (Secure Socket Layer) encryption, to achieve the highest level of security. This advanced encryption helps prevent the unauthorized interception of your personal and financial information during transmission over the Internet, ensuring a secure online shopping experience. We support the following card types: Klarna Shop now. Split your purchase into 4 interest-free payments with Klarna.* Spread the cost of your purchase over time by splitting it into 4 equal payments. Simply enter your credit or debit card details at checkout, and automatic payments will be taken every two weeks. There is no interest or additional fees when you pay on time. Requirements for using Klarna at GARVEE: 1.Klarna is only available to customers with a US resident address. 2.Klarna may only be used on purchases made on garvee.com. Learn more about Klarna: Klarna Policy Klarna Terms and Conditions For any more info for Klarna, just click here . Further Payment Notes Please do not send cash. Please do not send cheques or money orders. Please do not email us details of bank accounts or credit cards (unless it is instructed by us, in which case please closely follow our instructions). We cannot accept Western Union money transfers. We also do not accept other online payment systems except the ones we mentioned above. Please do not attempt to use another person's credit card or PayPal account for payments, unless you are certain the credit card/PayPal owner will be able to provide us with direct confirmation that the charge is authorized.
