SMS/RCS Opt-Out and Customer Care You may stop receiving marketing SMS or RCS messages at any time by replying STOP to a GARVEE message. After that confirmation, you will no longer receive marketing messages from the applicable messaging program unless you subsequently provide consent again. For help, text HELP for help or contact us at support@garvee.com . Opting out of marketing SMS or RCS messages does not necessarily prevent GARVEE from sending non-promotional communications relating to your orders, account, payments, shipping, returns, refunds, warranty, security, or customer service, where those communications are permitted by law or separately authorized. For clarity, this service-provider exception includes Klaviyo and other providers that process SMS or RCS information solely to deliver, administer, support, secure, analyze, or maintain our messaging programs. It does not permit those providers to use your messaging consent for unrelated marketing by other brands.
