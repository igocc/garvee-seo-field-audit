Warranty Policy Warranty Period GARVEE provides a 12-month repair warranty against defects in quality for all products purchased from garvee.com. This warranty period commences from the day the package is received by the customer. The Warranty provided covers the original purchaser and is valid for a period of 12 months, as evidenced by the original purchase receipt or other acceptable proof of purchase. Warranty Coverage Our Warranty covers the following: Defects in materials and workmanship under normal use and service. Faults that occur despite proper usage and care in accordance with our product instructions. This Warranty does not cover the following: Normal wear and tear or aging of the product,does not affect the fuction, such as damage to the appearance. We provide free replacement on product accessories within 30 days after delivery. Damage resulting from misuse, neglect, accidents, impact, water damage, or unauthorized modifications or repairs. Cosmetic damage that does not affect the functionality of the product. Loss or theft of the product. Other reasons not caused by our fault Making a Claim To make a warranty claim, please follow these steps: Contact our customer service team email: support@garvee.com with a detailed description of the issue and evidence of the defect (e.g., photos or videos). We will repair, replace, or refund, at our discretion, any product found to be covered by our Warranty.
